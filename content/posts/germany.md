Well, Mrs Badger and I have been talking about an extended EU trip for quite a few years now. We had initially planned on renting a flat for ~3 mo one summer, working remote, and exploring Europe. <h3 id="theme-parks">Theme parks?</h3><p>We went to the Deutchland Legoland and <em>dominated</em> the Firefighting competition. If you haven't had the joy of this competition, it is a group based challenge where you pile into a "fire engine" that is pump/piston powered. You have to move it about 100' to the other side of the play area, where there is a pump powered water cannon and "fire" in the windows of a fake building. Once a sufficient amount of water has been pumped through the window, the "fire" drops out of view signalling it is time to pile back in and book it back across the play area. I'm proud to say, we made it back to the start line before the next group even finished putting out their fire. The kids were less impressed than Mrs Badger and I, for some strange reason...</p><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card"><img src="/images/2024/01/IMG_7037.jpeg" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1536" height="2048"></figure><p>We also were able to make it down to Disneyland Paris, which was a really cool experience. I'm a fan!</p><h3 id="whats-next">Whats next?</h3><p>We are here through ~June of 2024, and will be heading back to the 208 at that time.
